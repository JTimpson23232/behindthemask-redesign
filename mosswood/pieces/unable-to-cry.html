Emotional Pain

Unable to Cry

Sometimes the pain is so deep it moves past tears into something heavier and harder to name. That is not numbness. That is depth.

Theme Pain past tears
Medium Original drawing
Year 2025
A note on this piece

There is a kind of grief that does not come out as tears. The body just gets heavy. The hands can't quite remember what they were going to do. You sit on the edge of the bed for thirty minutes without noticing the time. People around you ask if you are okay, and the word "okay" feels like a foreign language.

"Not crying is not the same as not feeling. Some pain moves past tears into something heavier, slower, and harder to explain."

This piece is for the people whose grief settled in their bones instead of their eyes. The people who got told they were "handling it well" because nothing visibly broke. The people who would have given anything to be able to just cry it out and be done.

You are not numb. You are not broken. You are not failing at grief. You are carrying something the body decided was too heavy to release through tears. That is its own kind of holding, and you deserve company in it.
