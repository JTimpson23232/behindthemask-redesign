Identity

Authenticity

A reminder that the face you carry alone is just as real as the one you show the world. Both are you. Both belong here.

Theme Identity, the inner face
Medium Original drawing
Year 2025
A note on this piece

This piece was drawn for the people who spend a lifetime alternating between two faces and have started to feel like neither one belongs to them. The public face, the one we hand out at the door of every relationship. And the quieter one, the face nobody asks about, the one we carry alone in a room with no audience.

"You are not the public face you've learned to keep up. You are also not only the quiet, harder face you carry alone. Both belong to you. Both are real. Both are welcome here."

Most of us never get to put both faces on the same page in front of someone and have them say: yes, both. I made this piece because I needed someone to say that to me, and nobody did. So if you found it, you found it for a reason.

You don't have to choose between them. You don't have to perform the public face to be lovable, and you don't have to hide the quieter face to be welcome. This is the kind of place where both can rest.
