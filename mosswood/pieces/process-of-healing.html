Healing

Process of Healing

Healing is not the bright arc the inspirational quotes promised. It is layered, slow, and still worth every step.

Theme Healing, the unmarketed kind
Medium Original drawing
Series Process of Healing · six pieces
A note on this piece

The version of healing that gets sold is bright and linear. You get sad, you do the work, you come out the other side, you post about it. That version did not match any of the actual healing I have ever done, or watched anyone close to me do.

"Healing is layered. It moves in spirals, not arrows. You can be three years out and still have a Tuesday afternoon that knocks you down."

Real healing is slow, layered, and sometimes invisible. It happens in the small unflattering moments. The Tuesday afternoon you cried in your car. The phone call you finally made. The boundary you said out loud for the first time. The morning you forgave yourself for an old thing you didn't even know you'd been carrying.

This piece is for everyone in the middle of it. You are not behind. You are not failing the timeline. The slow part of healing is also part of healing.
