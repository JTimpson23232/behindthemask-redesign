Grief & Change

Seasons

Every season of suffering carries the seed of the next season of growth. Not as a promise. As a slow fact, only visible in retrospect.

Theme Grief, change, return
Medium Original drawing series
Series Seasons · four pieces
A note on this piece

Nobody who has lived through a hard winter wants to be told it is just a season. It is true and it is cold comfort. The trees do not need to be told the spring is coming. They are already doing the unglamorous work of staying alive under the snow.

"You are not behind. You are wintering. The work of wintering is not the work of spring, and it is not less important."

This piece is for the people in a hard season. Not to rush you out of it. Just to keep you company in it. The work you are doing right now is the unglamorous, invisible work of staying alive. That work counts. That work matters. That work is enough.

You do not need to be in spring to belong here. You are welcome in any season. The room you are sitting in will still be here when the light changes.
