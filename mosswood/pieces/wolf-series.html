Strength

The Wolf Series

What looks dangerous from the outside is often just something that learned to protect itself. The wolf was never the villain. She was the one who survived.

Theme Strength, the protective kind
Medium Original drawing series
Series The Wolf · six pieces
A note on this series

Most people who have been hurt early learn very young to grow teeth. Not because they are cruel. Because the world they were handed required it. The world looked at the teeth and named the wolf dangerous, and never asked what she was protecting.

"The wolf is not the villain in this story. She is the part of you that decided you were worth protecting, on the days no one else did."

This series is for the people who have been told they are too much, too sharp, too defensive, too quick to leave. For the people who learned to bare teeth because that is what kept them alive. The wolf is not a flaw. The wolf is the part of you that decided you were worth keeping.

You do not have to apologize for surviving. You do not have to file your teeth down to be loved well. The right people will recognize the wolf and stay anyway.
