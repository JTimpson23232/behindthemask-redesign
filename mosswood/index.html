A note from the artist

I made this in the kind of quiet the woods give back.

The project began on nights when nothing in the bright rooms made sense. The work is honest. It is also old. The kind of old that says: this has been carried before, and you are not the first to set it down.

You are not strange for feeling what you feel. You are human. You are welcome here.
