Behind the Mask · Series Concepts

Four directions for the
Infertility series.

Same content. Same tone. Four different rooms for the work to live in.

Every concept shows the same three placeholder pieces with the same bracketed story slots. The actual artwork and Priscilla's written story will drop into whichever direction she chooses. The "painful truth confession" portion stays unpublished until the closing piece is finished.

The brief: raw but not hopeless, honest, real and emotionally safe, creative and artistic. Someone survived something here — not look how tortured I was.

How to choose.

Open each one. Scroll all the way through. Don't read the placeholder text — feel the room. Notice which one feels most like the story you wrote when you read it back to yourself.

Editorial if you want the words to lead. Journal if you want it to feel hand-made and personal. Sanctuary if you want the art to be the cathedral. Witness if you want art and words to be equal partners on the page.

There's no wrong answer. Tell us which room feels like yours.
