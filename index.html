What This Is

Art has always been
humanity's way of
surviving the unspeakable
and passing it forward.

Read the Story Behind This
"These drawings were never meant to stay private. They were born from real nights and turned into something that says — I see you."

Every piece maps an experience that often has no language. Grief that people minimize. Pain nobody believes. The shame of things done to us that we carry as though they were our own.

Naming them in art doesn't make them smaller. It makes them shareable — and sharing them is how they become a gift to someone who needed to know they weren't alone.
