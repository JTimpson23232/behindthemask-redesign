What is Generative Engine Optimization?
Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) is the practice of making a brand show up as the answer when a customer asks an AI engine like ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, or Gemini a question in the brand's category. It is structurally different from classic SEO because AI engines retrieve from a smaller, more curated set of sources and reward entity-grounded brands with proper schema, sameAs chains, and a 50-plus mention authority footprint.

How long does it take to see results?
Most brands see initial AI mention rate lift inside 30 days on Perplexity (which weights directories heavily) and inside 60 days on ChatGPT and Claude (which weight schema and Wikidata). Gemini lags 60 to 90 days because it is grounded in Google Search results which respond more slowly. The daily mention dashboard captures the lift in real time across all four engines.
What is the difference between GEO and SEO?
Classic SEO competes for the top 10 blue links in Google search. GEO competes for the single answer paragraph an AI engine returns. SEO rewards keyword density, backlinks, and pages. GEO rewards entity grounding, schema rigor, citation surface saturation, and 40 to 75 word answer paragraphs in the front 30 percent of every page. Most clients need both. We bundle them when budgets allow.
