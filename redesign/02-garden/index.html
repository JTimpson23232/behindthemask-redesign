a little about this garden

Nothing here is meant to shock you, or pull you under.

Every piece is drawn from a real experience. Many of them ones I lived, and many that friends and readers asked me to give shape to. None of them are here to perform pain. They are here to keep company.

Walk slowly. Skip what doesn't call to you. Linger where it does. The garden will be here when you come back.

the beds
