Manifesto

Nothing here is here to perform pain, or to pull you under.

Every piece in this archive begins with a real experience. Many of them ones I lived. Many that readers and friends asked me to give shape to. None of them are made to shock you.

Browse silently. Skip what doesn't call to you. Linger where it does. You don't owe this site anything. You are welcome here regardless.

Essay · A note from the artist

I started drawing the things I couldn't say.

The project began as private. A handful of drawings made on nights I needed somewhere for an unspeakable thing to go. Then friends wrote in. They had things they had needed pictures for, and never found them.

So this archive is what we made together. Every piece begins with a real experience, given to me, and is returned to anyone else who needed to know they were not alone in it.

You are not strange for feeling what you feel. You are human. You are welcome here.

— The Artist
