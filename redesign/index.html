Behind the Mask · Full Redesign

Four ways to make the site
feel safe instead of evil.

Same artist. Same work. Four completely different rooms for someone in pain to walk into.

Every concept is engineered around one rule: this place should feel like hospitality, not exhibition. None of them open with shock imagery. None of them make the visitor explain themselves at the door. Each one solves "safe" in a different way.

The brief: real, emotionally safe, but creative and artistic. Make people feel held when they get here, not approached with dread. Someone survived something here. Not "look how tortured I was."

Concept 03
Lantern
I'll sit with you in the dark.

Warm umber lit from within. Candle-glow amber accents. For visitors who find bright/cheerful sites invalidating in the middle of something hard. Anti-toxic-positivity. Dignified low light, not gothic. The site refuses to flinch.

Concept 04
Letter
I wrote this for you.

Paper white, large serif body, handwriting accents. The homepage IS a letter from the artist to the reader. Almost no UI chrome. Art appears mid-letter pinned like a postcard. The most intimate of the four. Reading replaces browsing.

How to choose, without overthinking it.

Open each one. Scroll the whole thing. Don't read the placeholder copy too closely. Notice which room makes your shoulders drop.

Hearth if you want it to feel like a friend's living room. Garden if you want it to feel like healing has its own pace. Lantern if you want it to refuse to be peppy. Letter if you want it to feel like opening mail from someone who gets it.

All four refuse the "look how tortured I was" energy. They each get there a different way. Tell me which room feels like yours and we build it out fully.
