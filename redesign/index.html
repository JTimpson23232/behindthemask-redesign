Behind the Mask · Six Redesign Concepts

Six rooms for the work
to live in.

Same artist. Same gallery. Six genuinely different palettes and feels.

The first round of concepts was too similar (cream, beige, paper-warm). This round breaks the palette entirely. Each concept now sits in a different temperature, brightness, and emotional register. The brief still holds: hospitality, not exhibition. Real, emotionally safe, creative and artistic.

The new palette spread: warm-light (Hearth), cool-light (Tidewater), warm-dark (Lantern), cool-dark (Iris), modern-bright (Atrium), deep-earth (Mosswood). No two share DNA.

Concept 05 · Modern-bright

How to choose, without overthinking it.
