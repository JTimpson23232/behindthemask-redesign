a note from the artist

I made this because I needed it too.

I started drawing the things I couldn't say. Pieces of life I'd been told to put away or be quieter about. Some I wasn't even sure I was allowed to feel.

If one of these meets you where you are, then it found the place it was made for. You are not strange for feeling what you feel. You are human. You are welcome here.

- the artist
