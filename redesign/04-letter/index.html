An open letter From the artist
If you found this, read first.

I made this whole place so you wouldn't have to explain yourself at the door.

If you've arrived here, something in your life is asking to be witnessed. That's enough of a reason to be welcome. You do not have to be in crisis, and you do not have to be "over it." You can just be here.

First, a few things I want you to know
Nothing on this site exists to shock you. Heavy pieces are tucked behind soft doors, and you choose what you open. You can leave anytime. The hard work is the art; the meeting it should be quiet.

I started drawing the things I couldn't say out loud. Things I had been told to keep small, or that nobody around me wanted to look at directly. Over time, friends started writing in. They were asking for pieces about their own quiet hurts. So that's what this site is now. A place where the unmet experiences finally get drawn.

  • If you arrived overwhelmed, the gallery is below. Walk one piece at a time, then close the tab.
  • If you arrived curious, browse by feeling, not by intensity. Nothing here is here to hurt you.
  • If you arrived carrying something, write me at the end of this letter. I read every one.

Here's a piece I want you to see first. Not because it is the heaviest. Because it is the most honest about what this place is doing.

I'll be honest: I made this site because I needed it first. The version of healing that gets sold to people in pain is bright and noisy and constantly trying to cheer them up. That version did not work for me. What worked was someone refusing to flinch.

So this is the room I would have wanted. Low lights. Honest art. No pressure to be okay yet. The rest of the letter is short.

Stay as long as you need. I'm glad you're here.
— the artist
