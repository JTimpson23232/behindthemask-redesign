A note from the artist

I made the kind of room I would have wanted on the hardest nights.

Quiet enough that you can hear yourself. Cool enough that nothing in the room is asking you to perform brightness. The work is honest, but never hostile.

You don't have to be okay yet to be here. You don't have to have it figured out. You can just be here.

— The Artist
