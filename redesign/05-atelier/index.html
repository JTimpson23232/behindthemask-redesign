i.
Foreword

Each piece in this collection began as something that could not be said. Some of them my own. Many of them written in by readers who found this site and asked: would you give a form to this?

The work is honest. It is not, however, hostile to the viewer. Heavy pieces are tucked behind soft doors. You choose what you open and when. You are not asked to explain yourself at the entrance.

What you'll find here is not a record of suffering. It is a record of having been believed.

— The Artist · Behind the Mask

iii.
Essay

I started drawing the things I couldn't say.

The project began as private. A handful of drawings made on nights I needed somewhere for an unspeakable thing to go. Then friends wrote in. They had things they had needed pictures for, and never found them.

So this archive is what we made together. Every piece begins with a real experience, given to me, and is returned to anyone else who needed to know they were not alone in it.

You are not strange for feeling what you feel. You are human. You are welcome here.

— The Artist
