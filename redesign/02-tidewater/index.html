A note from the artist

I made this in the kind of quiet I needed first.

The project began on nights when I couldn't say a thing out loud and needed somewhere for it to go. Then readers wrote in. They had things they needed pictures for and had never found.

So the collection is what we've made together. Every piece begins with a real experience, given to me, and returned to anyone else who needed to know they weren't alone in it.

— The Artist
