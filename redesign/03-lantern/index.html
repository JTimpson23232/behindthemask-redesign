A note kept beside the lamp

I keep this place because someone kept one for me.

There was a season I couldn't be cheered up. The forwarded inspirational quotes felt like being told to smile while drowning. What I needed was somebody who wouldn't flinch.

So I started drawing the things people don't usually let us draw. Not to perform what I survived, but to mark that I did. And to leave the light on for whoever comes next.

- the artist
