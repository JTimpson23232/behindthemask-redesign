a note from the artist

I made this to be the kind of room I needed first.

Soft enough to enter without performing anything. Honest enough to name the hard things by their right names. Quiet enough that you can hear yourself.

If a piece finds you here, that means another person has lived something close to it. You are in good company at the edge of this thing.
