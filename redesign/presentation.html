Six full homepage redesigns. Same artist, same gallery, six completely different palettes and feels. Each one is engineered to feel safe, not on display.

The brief: real, emotionally safe, creative and artistic. Someone survived something here. Not "look how tortured I was." Every concept refuses shock imagery at the door. Heavy pieces sit behind soft doors. The visitor never has to explain herself to be welcome.

How to choose, without overthinking it: flip through. Don't read the placeholder words too closely. Notice which room makes your shoulders drop.

Concept 01
Hearth
"Come inside, the kettle's on."

Warm cream and soft peach. Rounded corners, soft pillows of color. Friendly humanist serif (Fraunces). The site opens by saying hello like a friend's living room. Artist appears as the host, not the survivor on display. A soft promise strip up top: "no pressure to share," "soft entries," "made with care."

Pick Hearth if you want it to feel like a friend's home, doors held open, the room ready for you.

Concept 02
Tidewater
"The fog will lift."

Cool fog white, slate, sea-glass green, pale coral, anchored by deep navy ink. Classical serif (Lora). Coastal dawn energy. The first cool-toned concept in the set. Calming by way of water and fog, not warmth. Architectural and spacious without being clinical.

Concept 03
Lantern
"I'll sit with you in the dark."

Warm umber lit from within by candle-glow amber. For visitors who find peppy bright sites invalidating in the middle of something hard. Anti-toxic-positivity. Dignified low light, not gothic. The site refuses to flinch and refuses to perform cheerfulness.

Palette
Warm-dark Vigil Honest
Pick Lantern if you want the site to refuse peppy. To meet whoever arrives where they actually are.

Concept 05
Atrium
"Honest by being clean."

Bright gallery white, slate, single warm ochre accent. Editorial-modern. Sans-serif primary (Inter) with a restrained serif headline (Newsreader). Aesop store or Frieze magazine energy. Refuses both melodrama and saccharine. Confident without being aggressive. The work treated with the calm authority of a contemporary gallery.

Palette
Modern Bright Considered
Pick Atrium if you want it to feel like a real contemporary gallery. Honest, clean, unsentimental.

Concept 06
Mosswood
"The woods knew first."

Deep forest green, birch, rust, mushroom. EB Garamond serif. Primordial, mossy, the kind of old that holds without needing to speak. The opposite of clean-modern Atrium. Earthy companion energy. For the visitor who needs the room to feel ancient and known rather than designed.

A short note on choosing

You don't have to decide today. Just notice which one your shoulders prefer.

All six are real, full builds. Whichever you pick, we can show you a working preview in the browser, polish the details with you, then port it across the entire site.
